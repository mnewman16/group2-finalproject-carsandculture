Movies that highlight Cars

There are a ton of movies that highlight cars. They do a great job of providing entertainment and helping to develop next generation of gearheads. Here are a few of our favorites, and some honorable mentions.

Pixar's Cars series

Pixar's Cars series is an exciting animated kids movie that follows Lightning McQueen. He's a racecar who gets stranded in a small town, which forces him to reflect on life and his priorities. This forced reflection helps him realize that winning isn't the only thing that matters in life. The series includes lessons that kids can reflect on, while keeping them engaged with the bright colors and humor.

Image via Disney / Pixar

The Fast & Furious franchise

The Fast & Furious franchise follows Dominic Toretto and his crew as they navigate a world of street racing, heists, and high-stakes action. Originating as an underground street racing film in 2001, the series now spans ten main movies with several spinoffs. Car culture is front and center throughout the entire series.

Image via Fast & Furious Wiki / Universal Pictures

Honorable Mentions

Some honorable mentions for movies that highlight cars include F1 and Ford v Ferrari. Both films highlight the peak performance that cars are capable of, featuring rivalries, high-speed races, and beautiful cars.
