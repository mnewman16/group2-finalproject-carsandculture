Toy Cars

While there is a plethora of brands providing toy cars, here we will focus on a few of the biggest players.

Hot Wheels

Hot Wheels has been one of the most iconic toy cars in the world since its debut in 1968. The brand has expanded since starting off with simple toy cars, now offering collector editions, video games, and even a Netflix series. This truly makes Hot Wheels a cornerstone of car culture for kids of all ages.

Image via Mattel

Remote Controlled Cars

Remote controlled cars have been an influential part of many childhoods for decades. They provide kids, teens, and adults alike the opportunity to control various models of cars, from sports cars to trucks and everything in between. RC cars provide a great chance to practice skills such as steering, braking, and throttle control, making them an excellent entry into the car scene.

Image via Traxxas

Tonka

Tonka trucks, modeled after real construction and utility vehicles, give kids a hands-on way to engage with the world of heavy equipment and machinery. Their signature yellow dump trucks and construction vehicles are some of the most recognizable toys in history, even to this day.

Image via Tonka trucks
