Video Games

As a gamer, you have multiple choices for video games focusing on cars and racing. Here we will showcase some of those options...

Forza

Forza is a great choice for the die-hard car enthusiast. With tons of cars and customization options and a vast open-world environment, Forza will provide an enormous amount of enjoyment. Race and compete with your favorite cars and crush the competition today!

Image via Forza / Xbox Game Studios

Mario Kart

For everyone from kids and casual gamers to the most serious competitors, one game sure to keep you entertained is Mario Kart! You can customize your vehicle each round and pick the options that best suit your playstyle. With tons of tracks to play on and characters to unlock, Mario Kart is a great video game that focuses on racing and healthy competition for everyone!

Image via Nintendo

Honorable Mentions

A few other car-focused games you can enjoy include Need for Speed, Gran Turismo, and Rocket League. Need for Speed lets you get into all kinds of shenanigans, race across tracks and run from the law. Gran Turismo is a more authentic car racing simulation. Rocket League mixes soccer with cars in its own unique way. All are great games that can be enjoyed for hours on end!

Image via Rocket League / Epic Games
