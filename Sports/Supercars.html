Supercars

Supercars are high-performance, street-legal vehicles that prioritize extreme speed, advanced engineering, and striking, aerodynamic designs over everyday practicality. Often produced in limited numbers by specialized manufacturers, they serve as a benchmark for automotive technology and status, bridge the gap between sports cars and track-only racers.

Ferrari

Ferrari is an Italian luxury sports car manufacturer based in Maranello. The company is known for its high-performance, limited-production vehicles that combine cutting-edge technology with stunning design.

Image via Sotheby's Realty

Lamborgini

Lamborghini is an Italian luxury sports car manufacturer based in Sant'Agata bolognese. The company is known for its high-performance, exotic vehicles that combine aggressive design with cutting-edge technology.

Image via Hot Cars

Other players in the Supercar Game

Porsche is a German manufacturer renowned for its precision engineering and the iconic 911, which serves as a global benchmark for balancing daily usability with track performance. Their high-end models, such as the GT3 RS, utilize advanced active aerodynamics and high-revving engines to deliver a pure, driver-focused experience. Koenigsegg is a boutique Swedish brand that pushes the absolute limits of physics through in-house innovations like camless engines and single-speed transmissions. They specialize in "megacars" designed to shatter world speed records while maintaining a high level of artisanal craftsmanship
