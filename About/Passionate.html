Why We Are Passionate About Cars

Michael's Passion for Cars

Michael has always enjoyed tearing things apart and putting them back together to learn how they work. While his parents purchased a reliable Honda Accord for his first car, he quickly traded it in for a convertible Ford Mustang. That Mustang quickly turned into a project car, replacing almost everything about it except the frame. Nowadays, that car provides smiles per gallon, while he drives a Dodge Charger SXT as a daily reliable car. The Mustang is still his baby, and when it's not being tinkered with, he loves any opportunity to take it out for a drive. Before getting married, he loved the thrill and adrenaline of a good joy ride.

Michael's current car / Hover for dream car

Will's Passion for Cars

Will Has always been passionate about cars when he was playing with Hot Wheels as a kid. From a young age Will has always loved cars and trucks and has traded Hot Wheels with friends for years. He also enjoys playing racing games in his free time.
