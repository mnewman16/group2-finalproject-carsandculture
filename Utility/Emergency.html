Emergency Vehicles

Police Cars

Police cars are one of the most recognizable vehicles on the road. Equipped with red and blue flashing lights, these cars are built for rapid emergency response. Modern engines provide them with the power for high-speed pursuits, and computers and communication equipment ensure that officers are able to coordinate with one another to best serve the community.

Image via City of Huntsville

Fire Trucks

Fire trucks are one of the most iconic emergency vehicles in the world. With their sirens and flashing lights, they're able to get to the source of an emergency and provide relief for fires and accidents. Equipped with ladders, hoses, and thousands of gallons of water, they truly are essential to our society.

Image via City of Huntsville

Ambulances

Ambulances are built to quickly transport sick, injured, and dying patients to hospitals as safely as possible. Equipped with life-saving medical equipment and staffed by paramedics, these vehicles are essential to society, ensuring our friends and loved ones can get the urgent care they need in time.

Image via HEMSI
