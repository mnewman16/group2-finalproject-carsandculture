Commuting & Transportation

Buses

Buses are one of the most popular forms of public transportation to this day. Most cities provide buses that can get you around efficiently, providing a great option for those without a personal vehicle.

Image via Greyhound

Trains

Trains have been an important part of our country's transportation infrastructure since the 19th century. They are still one of the most efficient ways to move large numbers of people and cargo across long distances.

Image via Amtrak

EV/Hybrid Commuting Cars

Electric and hybrid vehicles are becoming increasingly popular for those looking to save money at the pump and reduce their environmental impact. While not all cities are equipped to handle EVs, more and more are building charging stations to help support them going forward.

Image via Tesla
